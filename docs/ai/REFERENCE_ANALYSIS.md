# Official product reference analysis

Research date: 2026-07-14. Only official public product/help pages were used. These references inform product behavior; they do not authorize copying visual identity, layout, wording, assets, logistics, payments, or commercial terms.

## PangoBooks

Official evidence:

- [Sell books on PangoBooks](https://pangobooks.com/sell/) presents a book-specific fast-listing sequence: photograph the actual copy, scan ISBN/barcode to prefill metadata, add condition/details and price, and publish into a seller bookstore.
- [Listing and shop guidelines](https://help.pangobooks.com/en/articles/6060563-pangobooks-listing-and-shop-guidelines) emphasize actual physical-copy photos, honest condition/flaw disclosure, and book-focused seller shops.
- [Shelves on PangoBooks](https://help.pangobooks.com/en/articles/8651793-what-are-shelves-on-pangobooks) distinguish saving a particular copy from saving a title, support public/private shelf intent, and connect wishlists with availability/price awareness.
- [PangoBooks bookstores](https://pangobooks.com/bookstore) make seller inventory and reader identity discoverable.
- [Bundle search](https://pangobooks.com/bundle-search) supports finding multiple desired books from one seller.

Adopt or adapt:

- Treat a book title/edition and an individual physical copy as related but distinct concepts.
- Keep listing creation fast while offering rich optional metadata.
- Require photos and condition detail about the actual copy.
- Separate saved listings from saved titles; support reader shelves and wanted titles.
- Give sellers coherent public bookstores and enable same-seller discovery.
- Balance transactional catalog behavior with reader identity and book culture.

Reject:

- US-specific shipping, payments, commissions, protection language, copy, branding, and layouts.
- Making every metadata field compulsory or assuming an ISBN exists.

## Vinted UK

Official evidence:

- [Managing listings](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/4/1574-managing-listings) exposes a clear listing lifecycle including editing, hiding/deleting, reserving, offers, and buyer/seller chat.
- [Selling multiple items as a set](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/3/257-selling-multiple-items-as-a-set?access_channel=hc_topics) stresses accurate photos/title/description, a clear set price, and negotiation.
- Vinted's official UK help structure groups selling, buying, messages, favorites, safety, reporting, and account controls into task-focused mobile flows.

Adopt or adapt:

- Short mobile-first listing path, explicit listing state, obvious edit/hide/sold/delete controls.
- Clear favorites, seller profile, message, report, and negotiation entry points.
- Responsive navigation that prioritizes browse, sell, saved items, messages, and profile.
- Trust and reporting guidance beside relevant decisions, not only in footer documents.

Reject:

- Fashion-oriented taxonomy/appearance, copied layouts/assets/branding, integrated payments/shipping, buyer-protection claims, and reservation semantics before BookSwap defines them.
- Treating “offer” and “exchange” as the same concept.

## Tap.az book category

Official evidence:

- [Tap.az books and magazines category](https://tap.az/elanlar/hobbi-ve-asude/kitab-ve-jurnallar) uses AZN, city, category filters, favorites, listing views, and generic classified cards.
- [An official Tap.az book listing](https://tap.az/elanlar/hobbi-ve-asude/kitab-ve-jurnallar/43553215) illustrates familiar local classified actions such as favorite, report, location, seller contact, and view count.

Local baseline:

- Keep AZN, city/region, direct seller contact, negotiability, simple posting, favorites, and reporting recognizable.
- Improve on generic categorization with author/language/ISBN/edition/condition details, physical-copy clarity, title identity, shelves, wanted books, and exchange intent.
- Do not import unrelated category leakage, unstructured descriptions, or precise contact/location exposure as the primary discovery model.

## Lalafo Azerbaijan book category

Official evidence:

- [Lalafo Azerbaijan books search](https://lalafo.az/azerbaijan/knigi-zhurnaly-cd-dvd/q-lalafo-kitab) shows AZN, cities/regions, “Razılaşma yolu ilə” negotiability, book subcategories, direct messages/calls, seller response indicators, and multi-book offers.

Local baseline:

- Use natural Azerbaijani negotiation and location terminology.
- Consider seller response context and bundles only after core trust data is reliable.
- Enforce book-only taxonomy and physical-book rules: generic results can mix unrelated goods and risky digital/PDF offers into book categories.

## BookSwap synthesis

| Need            | Adopt                                                | BookSwap-specific adaptation                                                    |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fast listing    | PangoBooks metadata assist; Vinted mobile simplicity | Essential physical-copy fields first; optional book depth; Azerbaijani defaults |
| Discovery       | Book-native title/copy model                         | Azerbaijani/Russian/English search, local location, sale/exchange intention     |
| Reader identity | Seller bookstore and shelves                         | Public seller inventory plus privacy-explicit owned/read/wanted shelves         |
| Saving          | Favorites and title wishlists                        | Preserve listing favorites; add title saves and wanted list separately          |
| Negotiation     | Clear message/offer entry                            | Direct protected chat; price negotiability and book exchange are distinct       |
| Exchange        | Not supplied by references as the core               | Privacy-safe two-way offered/wanted matching is BookSwap's differentiator       |
| Trust           | Actual photos, condition, profiles, reports          | No payment/delivery guarantees; eligible reviews, moderation, local safety copy |
| Local fit       | AZN, city, direct arrangement                        | Approximate area/handoff preferences; no precise public address                 |

The product should feel like the existing BookSwap bookstore, not a reskinned reference. The references validate behaviors and information architecture only.
