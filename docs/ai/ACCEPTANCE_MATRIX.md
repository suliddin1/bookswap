Updated: 28 July 2026

Status values: **PASS** = direct evidence exists; **PREPARED/BLOCKED** = deterministic implementation exists but external input prevents the decisive run; **PRODUCTION** = can only be verified after deployment; **POST-LAUNCH** = outside MVP.

## Authorization matrix

“Moderator” is intentionally not a privileged database role in the launch schema. A moderator-labeled ordinary account has normal-user permissions and cannot call admin/moderation actions. All current moderation state changes are admin-only and audited; a future moderator role requires an additive role/permission design.

## Strong pre-launch recommendations

- Docker-backed zero-to-head local reset and both SQL scripts.
- Approved networked dependency advisory scan.
- Native Azerbaijani editorial and qualified legal review.
- Recovery/incident tabletop and isolated restore exercise.
- Browser verification against the real development backend after the authorization key is supplied.

## Post-launch acceptance

Automated exchange/wanted matching, sale/exchange/both intention, shelves, social reading, complete edition metadata, external search infrastructure, integrated payment/escrow/shipping, and product analytics are not launch gates.
